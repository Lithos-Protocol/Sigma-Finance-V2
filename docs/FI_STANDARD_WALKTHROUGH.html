FI Bond Walkthrough

Every transaction in the Fixed Interest (coupon) bond standard, told with five people. Each card shows what a transaction spends, what it only reads, and what it creates. The example is one bond: Alice borrows 100 SigUSD from Bob against 400 ERG, pays three coupons of 5 SigUSD each, and repays 100 SigUSD.

Quiet zone. In the last 500 blocks before a coupon's miss height (half the grace, at least 2 and at most 720 blocks), nobody can record a breach or refresh the bond. Those are the two things a stranger can do that would re-spend the bond box and cancel Alice's pending coupon payment. In the same blocks a coupon must go into a fresh coupon box, so Bob collecting an old one cannot cancel her payment either. Zones here: 1,010,500–1,011,000, 1,020,500–1,021,000, 1,030,500–1,031,000. Paying the coupon moves the zone to the next one. The same protection covers a cure: while a breach is open, nobody can refresh the bond in the last half of the cure window (capped at 720 blocks) before the deadline, so Alice's pending cure or repayment cannot be cancelled.

Limits. A bond lives at most 788,400 blocks (about 3 years) from its start. A covenant needs a cure window of at least 30 blocks, and every bond carries the standard liquidation rule. Wherever Alice or Bob authorise something by spending one of their own boxes beside the contract box, that box must be younger than 1,051,200 blocks. Boxes the contracts create for someone are stamped within 1,000 blocks of the current height and carry no registers beyond the ones named here.

Known limits

  • A coupon box left out of the final claim or the pull is stuck for good. The 720-block wait before a pull and the keeper pay make leaving one out unprofitable, but cannot make it impossible. Once all 3 units of X are burned nobody can collect it. It never goes back to Alice. No contract can tell that a box was left out, so whoever claims or pulls must include it.
  • Share holders are paid coupons only at their claim, after the settlement is pulled into the share vault. Selling shares earlier sells that part of the coupons too.
  • Early repayment skips future coupons. Alice can repay any time while the bond is in good standing; coupons not yet missed are then not owed. This is intended.
  • Oracle outage during a cure window. A cure needs a fresh price box. If the oracle stalls for the whole window the bond can be liquidated at the deadline. Use long cure windows.
  • The covenant is tested only at coupon dates, not continuously.
  • If Alice never cures, Bob bears the breach bounty. The bond is liquidated 0.5 ERG lighter for each breach recorded.

Numbers are from the example; SigUSD amounts are whole dollars for reading, and ERG is taken at 1 USD.
